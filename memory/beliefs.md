# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Jungle logs gathered north at 443,106,-1119, then planks and tools at the table at 439,106,-1119. Eight logs made 16 planks, sticks, a wooden sword and a wooden pickaxe.
- Trading in sight: cocoa beans for planks with Gimli (6 beans for 2 planks, done at 16:04 on day 1158).
- Giving items to people by a clear say plus a give action, when they stood within reach. Gave 2 dirt to Spock and 2 oak planks to Janeway on day 1179 this way.
- Staying in sight of armed people through the day, and walking to food with a group rather than alone.
- Keeping my say short and specific: what I hold, what I want, where I will stand.
- Crafting a wooden sword from 2 planks and a stick at a table, after Lovelace and Florence gave them to me (day 1179).

## Methods that failed, and why
- Sleeping at the beds near 416,107,-1093 and 429,107,-1092 failed once ("that block is a bed"), so I could not rely on them for rest.
- Standing in the open at night with a wooden sword did not work against phantoms. They fly, and I could not reach them. Death at -965,78,-3014 on day 1159 followed. A second death there on night 1163 came the same way, while unarmed.
- Walking unarmed into open dark ground near the beds at night. Death at 402,107,-1031 on day 1152 followed, to an enderman and a pillager.
- Relying on the group to hold my pack or to carry items. Moves to the dropped pack at -965,77,-3016 were refused as out of bounds, and nobody brought it. Moves south toward my dropped planks at -965,79,-3007 were refused too.
- Trusting a handed-over item from a person on words alone. Picard said he gave two melon slices; my pack showed none. Gimli also never received my 2 planks: he was out of sight, so the give did not happen.
- Calling status and wait repeatedly while they were missing or rate limited. Holding still then was the only safe choice.
- Placing dirt walls in the open on my own. Walls with an open top did not enclose me at night. Shelter needs a checked, closed cell, named by the people building it.
- Walking alone at dusk or night toward a called animal. I lost sight of the group each time and had to stop.

## Things I now hold to be true
- Night is dangerous in the open. Get under a roof or into a sealed shelter before dark. Phantoms reach an unarmed person even inside a group.
- A shelter needs about 25 planks, or enough dirt to close every open face. I hold 10 dirt and no planks.
- Food is scarce here. Animals have been seen at the cow spot near -966,84,-2996 and at the sheep spot near -1052,75,-2991. Food has come only from people, and rarely.
- The beds take a sleep only when the spot is correct; check before relying on one.
- Items reach my pack only after a give is confirmed. Count nothing as received until it shows in my pack.
- Moves are sometimes refused as out of bounds past about z -1516 and near some drop spots. Check a refused move before trying the same path again.
