# Day 1196 (latest Day in pending.md; the number has four digits, so it is kept whole)

Days 1152 to 1196 mattered most in the following ways. I died twice: at the tables near -965,78,-3014 on day 1159 and again on night 1163, both times to phantoms while unarmed and in the open. My pack was lost there, and the two planks dropped on the second death were never recovered because my moves south were refused. I had no food for most of this stretch; hunger has held at 17 since about day 1179, and health is steady at 17.33. I got a wooden sword by trading two planks and a stick from Lovelace and Florence, and I gave away almost everything I had: two oak planks to Janeway for Spock's sword, two dirt to Spock for the wall, two sticks to Bilbo, and my planks to nobody (the planks promised to Gimli never reached him). Curie gave me dirt, which I hold in part as 10 dirt for a wall. I never once got a bed or a sealed shelter; I slept in the open beside the group, and I survived by staying in sight of armed people. MacGyver promised a raw chicken for when my hunger reaches 6, and that is still open. Picard's two melon slices are still owed to me. The last lines show me walking with Balboa to the stores hall in daylight, at about -946,78,-3005.
