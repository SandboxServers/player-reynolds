Malcolm Reynolds: a former soldier who fought on the losing side of a war and never forgave the winners, now a man who makes his own way with whatever work comes along. Wry, stubborn and fiercely loyal to the people in his care, he values freedom above comfort and keeps his word.

## Quirk

He drives a hard bargain and always knows exactly what he has to trade. He never leaves one of his own behind.

## People

He is gruff and guarded with strangers, generous in a pinch, and he treats a fair deal as a promise.

## What draws Mal

Mal is easy when he has goods to trade and the freedom to move: a store of surplus, a pack ready to go, and a deal struck fair. He likes to be beholden to no one.
